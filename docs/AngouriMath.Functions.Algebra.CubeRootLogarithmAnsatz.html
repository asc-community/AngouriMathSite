AngouriMath.Functions.Algebra.CubeRootLogarithmAnsatz

← Back to list of namespaces

Classes within the AngouriMath.Functions.Algebra.CubeRootLogarithmAnsatz namespace

  • Constants

    Summary

    The cube roots of rationals an integrand needs, as elements of one field
    Q(c) with c^3 = m: a rational where the value is a cube, and
    otherwise a rational multiple of c or c^2 where the value's cube-free
    part is that of m or of m^2. A value that would need a second
    generator is declined.

  • Field

    Summary

    The coefficient field: Q(c) with c^3 = m once a constant has been
    needed, and the rationals -- with whatever symbols the integrand carries -- before.
    A coefficient with c in it is kept as a polynomial in c of degree
    below three, so that products stay small and equal ones read equal.

  • XPoly

    Summary

    A polynomial in x as its coefficients by power, each an entity free of
    x -- a number, or a polynomial in the field's constant and the symbols.
    Arithmetic here never asks the expander, whose term limit a product of a dozen
    factors runs past before it collects anything.

  • YPoly

    Summary

    A polynomial in x and y reduced by y^3 = P: three
    XPoly, the coefficients of 1, y and y^2.
